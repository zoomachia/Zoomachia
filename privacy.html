Zoomachia tools: privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

"Zoomachia tools" is a private application used only by the owner of the Zoomachia channel to manage the channel's own accounts (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok): scheduling its own videos, reading its own analytics, and replying to comments posted on its own videos. It is not offered to the public and no other user can sign in to it.

What data the application accesses

  • Data of the Zoomachia accounts only: video metadata, analytics reports, and the public comments posted under Zoomachia's own videos, through the official YouTube API Services and the official Meta and Threads APIs.
  • Comments are read only to answer them on the channel's behalf. They are not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Where the data is kept

Access tokens are stored on the owner's own computer, in the Windows Credential Manager. Working files (reports, logs) stay on that computer. No data is sent to any third party other than the platforms themselves and, for posting to TikTok and Instagram, the scheduling service Zernio.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions and from the security settings of each platform.

YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
